Core finance formulas

A pocket sheet of the equations interviewers expect you to recall cold. Grounded in SEC Investor.gov and CFA TVM framing.

Time value of money

FV = PV × (1 + r)n

Future value of a lump sum compounding at rate r for n periods.

PV = FV ÷ (1 + r)n

Present value — what a future cash flow is worth today.

Years to double ≈ 72 ÷ (rate % )   // Rule of 72

SEC Investor.gov example: at ~9%, money doubles about every 8 years (72 ÷ 9).

Returns

Simple return = (Ending − Beginning) ÷ Beginning
Wealth multiple = Ending ÷ Beginning = 1 + return

Personal wealth

Net worth = Assets − Liabilities

From SEC Saving and Investing: list what you own vs what you owe.

Company snapshot ratios (interview staples)

Profit margin = Net income ÷ Revenue
Current ratio = Current assets ÷ Current liabilities
Debt-to-equity = Total debt ÷ Shareholders’ equity
P/E = Price per share ÷ Earnings per share

Cash-flow identity (balance sheet story)

Assets = Liabilities + Equity

Every asset is financed by someone else’s claim (debt) or the owners’ claim (equity).

Sources: Compound interest & Rule of 72, Saving and Investing, CFA TVM module.
