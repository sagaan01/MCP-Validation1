# 30-day Marketplace flip plan

## Days 1–3 — Market research (no buying)

- Find 20 comparable local listings.
- Record asking prices and how long each listing has been active.
- Identify five underpriced items.
- Do not buy yet.

## Days 4–7 — First purchases

- Buy 2–3 compact products after calling `evaluate_deal`.
- Inspect and test.
- Clean properly.
- Take 5–7 good photographs per item.
- Call `record_transaction` for each purchase.
- Keep the $85 reserve unless a standout deal appears.

## Days 8–14 — First sales

- List all products with competitive pricing.
- Respond quickly to messages.
- Track every offer and completed sale (`record_transaction` with status `sold`).
- Reinvest only money actually received.

## Days 15–21 — Reinvest

- Identify the fastest-selling category via `budget_status` + ledger.
- Buy more of that category; avoid slow movers.
- Aim for another 3–5 sales.

## Days 22–30 — Optimize

- Review actual net profit.
- Reduce prices on slow inventory.
- Name the best three products.
- Set next month’s inventory budget from cash on hand minus reserve.

## Sourcing checklist

Search phrases: Moving sale, Must sell today, Pickup today, Moving out, Garage sale, Office chair, Cordless drill, Free pickup, Under $25.

Sources: Facebook Marketplace, Buy Nothing (if rules allow), garage sales, estate sales, Craigslist Free, local community groups.
